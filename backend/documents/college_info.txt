Welcome to CollegeMate AI Knowledge Base.

1. General Information:
- College Name: Metropolitan Institute of Technology (MIT)
- Campus Location: Main Campus, Tech Park Road, Sector 5.
- Office Timings: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Monday to Friday).
- General Helpline: +1 (555) 019-2834
- Email: contact@mit.edu

2. Library Information:
- Working Hours: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM (Monday to Saturday), 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Sundays.
- Book Issued Limit: 4 books per student for 15 days.
- Overdue Fine: $0.50 per day per book.
- Digital Library: Accessible via portal.mit.edu/library with student ID credentials.

3. Attendance Rules:
- Minimum Requirement: 75% attendance is mandatory in each subject to be eligible to sit for final semester examinations.
- Medical Leave: Valid medical certificates must be submitted to the HOD (Head of Department) within 3 days of resuming classes. Maximum condonation allowed is 10%.

4. Exam and Grading:
- Internal Assessment (IA): 3 internal assessments are conducted per semester. The best 2 out of 3 scores are considered.
- Grading System: Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) on a 10-point scale. Passing mark is 40% in individual papers.

5. Hostels:
- Warden Contact: Chief Warden office can be reached at extension 402.
- Curfew: 9:30 PM for all hostel residents. Special permission required from the Warden for late entry.
- Visitors: Allowed only in the visitor lobby from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM on weekdays and 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekends.

6. Transportation:
- Route 1: Covers North Campus to Main Campus (Departs 7:30 AM).
- Route 2: Covers South City to Main Campus (Departs 7:45 AM).
- Bus Pass: Monthly pass can be obtained from the Finance Office for $30.
